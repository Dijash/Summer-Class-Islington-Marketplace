1. Information We Collect

We collect personal and technical information necessary to deliver seamless customer checkout and merchant services across MarketPlace:

Account & Profile

Full name, email address, phone number, and delivery shipping addresses.

Order Transactions

Payment receipts, order history, and product reviews submitted by users.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly utilized to process e-commerce orders, authenticate sessions, maintain platform security, detect fraudulent activities, and provide responsive customer service.

3. Third-Party Sharing & Partners

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to advertisers.

We only share necessary operational details with verified third-party partners required to fulfill your order (e.g., payment processing gateways and shipping fulfillment logistics).

4. Cookies & Session Storage

MarketPlace employs essential HTTP cookies and CSRF tokens to keep your session secure, remember items added to your shopping cart, and maintain user preferences.

5. Data Security Protection

We implement industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption, salted password hashing, database isolation, and routine security audits to prevent unauthorized data disclosure.

6. Your Rights & Data Access

You maintain full control over your personal data. You may request a copy of your stored records, edit your profile, or request account deletion by emailing our support desk.
